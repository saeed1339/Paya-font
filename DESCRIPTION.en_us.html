Paya is a Persian/Arabic typeface family designed by Saeed Rezaee. All Persian glyphs have been completely redesigned, while the font builds upon the foundations of the DejaVu Fonts and the Vazir template by Saber Rastikerdar.

Paya supports Persian, Arabic, and Latin scripts, and includes five weights: Thin, Light, Regular, Medium, and Bold. It features proper OpenType mark positioning and shaping for Arabic script, making it suitable for a wide range of text settings in right-to-left languages.
